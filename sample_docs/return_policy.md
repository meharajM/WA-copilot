# Returns and Exchange Policy

**Last Updated:** March 2026

## Overview
We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase! If you are not entirely happy, we're here to help. You have **30 calendar days** to return an item from the date you received it.

## Eligibility for Returns
To be eligible for a return:
- Your item must be unused, unwashed, and in the same condition that you received it.
- Your item must be in the original packaging.
- Your item needs to have the receipt or proof of purchase.
- All original tags must still be attached.

## Non-Returnable Items
Certain types of items cannot be returned, including:
- Perishable goods (e.g., food, flowers, or plants).
- Custom products (e.g., special orders or personalized items).
- Personal care goods (e.g., beauty products).
- Digital downloads or software licenses.

## Return Process
1. Contact our customer support team at **support@example.com** with your order number.
2. We will review your request and provide a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number.
3. Box up your item securely and write the RMA number visibly on the outside of the box.
4. Ship the package to:
   *Example Returns Dept.*
   *123 Logistics Way*
   *Commerce City, CA 90210*

## Refunds
Once we receive your item, we will inspect it and notify you that we have received your returned item. We will immediately notify you on the status of your refund after inspecting the item.

If your return is approved, we will initiate a refund to your credit card (or original method of payment). You will receive the credit within **5 to 7 business days**, depending on your card issuer's policies.

## Shipping Costs for Returns
- **Defective/Incorrect Items:** We will cover the return shipping costs. We will issue a prepaid shipping label.
- **Change of Mind:** You will be responsible for paying your own shipping costs for returning your item. Shipping costs are non-refundable.

## Exchanges
If your item is defective or damaged and you need to exchange it for the same item, please email us directly with a photo of the defect. We will process an exchange within 48 hours of verification.
