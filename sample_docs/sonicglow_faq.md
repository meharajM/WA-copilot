# SonicGlow Pro Smart Toothbrush - Product FAQ

## Product Overview
The SonicGlow Pro is our flagship smart toothbrush, featuring advanced AI brushing analysis, sonic vibration technology (40,000 VPM), and a beautifully minimal, waterproof design.

## Specifications
- **Battery Life:** Up to 30 days on a single charge.
- **Charging Time:** 4 hours from 0% to 100% using the included magnetic USB-C dock.
- **Vibration Speed:** 40,000 vibrations per minute.
- **Modes:** 
  1. *Clean*: Standard daily cleaning.
  2. *Sensitive*: For gentle cleaning and sensitive gums.
  3. *Whitening*: Pulsating mode to remove surface stains.
- **Water Resistance:** IPX7 (completely safe to use in the shower).
- **Connectivity:** Bluetooth 5.0 (Connects to the SonicGlow iOS and Android app).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. How do I pair the SonicGlow Pro with my phone?
Make sure Bluetooth is enabled on your phone. Open the SonicGlow app, turn on your toothbrush by pressing the power button once, and tap "Pair Device" in the app. The LED ring on the brush will pulse blue when pairing is successful.

### 2. My toothbrush isn't charging. What should I do?
First, ensure the magnetic charging base is plugged into a functional 5V USB wall adapter. Wipe the bottom of the toothbrush and the charging base with a dry cloth to remove any water or debris that might be interfering with the magnetic connection. If the LED breathing light doesn't turn on, please contact support.

### 3. How often should I replace the brush head?
Dentists recommend replacing your brush head every 3 months. The SonicGlow app will naturally track your usage and notify you when it's time to swap. You can purchase replacements at `www.example.com/refills`.

### 4. Is the toothbrush safe for braces?
Yes! The SonicGlow Pro is completely safe for braces, veneers, and dental implants. We recommend using the "Sensitive" mode if you have recently had dental work.

### 5. Why is the app telling me I am brushing too hard?
The SonicGlow Pro has a built-in pressure sensor. If you apply too much force, the handle will gently stutter and the LED ring will flash red. This protects your gums from recession.
